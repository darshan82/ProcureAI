# Master Supply Agreement: Developer Laptops (TND-1001)

Between ACME Corp ("Buyer") and the selected supplier ("Supplier"). Contact: procurement@acme.test, +1 415 555 0100.

## 1. Scope of supply
The Supplier shall deliver 120 developer laptops with 32GB RAM, 1TB SSD and a 14-inch display, pre-imaged with the Buyer's standard build, to the Buyer's Dubai and London offices.

## 2. Delivery
All units must be delivered within 30 calendar days of purchase order issue. Partial deliveries are allowed with prior written consent.

## 3. Warranty and support SLA
The Supplier provides a 3-year on-site warranty covering parts and labour. Critical hardware failures must be responded to within 4 business hours and resolved within 2 business days. A replacement device must be provided if a repair exceeds 3 business days.

## 4. Penalties and liquidated damages
Late delivery incurs liquidated damages of 0.5% of the order value per day of delay, capped at 10% of the total contract value. Repeated SLA breaches (more than 3 in a quarter) entitle the Buyer to a service credit of 5% of the quarterly support fee.

## 5. Payment terms
Payment is due net 45 days from receipt of a valid invoice and successful acceptance testing. 10% of the contract value is retained until the end of the first warranty year.

## 6. Evaluation criteria
Bids are scored on price (40%), warranty and support SLA (30%), delivery time (20%) and sustainability, including EPEAT Gold certification and take-back programme (10%).

## 7. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach with 30 days written notice if the breach is not remedied. The Buyer may terminate for convenience with 60 days notice.

## 8. Data protection
All devices returned for repair must have storage wiped to NIST 800-88 standard or the drive must be retained by the Buyer.
